Tariffia Wallet is a self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet for people who want direct control over their assets. Your private keys stay on your device. There is no account to create, no identity verification and no company holding your funds.

Designed for everyday use and long-term storage, Tariffia Wallet combines a clear portfolio view with advanced tools for Bitcoin, Ethereum, EVM networks and privacy-focused coins.

KEY FEATURES

- True self-custody
Create or restore a wallet with your recovery phrase. Keys are generated and stored on your device, not on a server.

- Multi-chain support
Manage Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana, TON, Zcash and other supported networks in one app.

- Bitcoin tools
SPV wallet, BIP44/49/84/86, custom fees, transaction timelocks and support for watch-only wallets.

- Privacy coins
Support for shielded Zcash transactions and privacy-focused workflows, including Tor when enabled.

- Multiple wallets and accounts
Organize funds into separate portfolios and accounts for spending, savings or different parts of your portfolio.

- WalletConnect
Connect to decentralized applications and use supported Web3 services without exposing your private keys.

- Market insights
Follow prices, charts, market capitalization, TVL and coin information in a focused interface.

- Clear transaction controls
Review fees, addresses and transaction details before you send. Adjust network fees when needed.

- No tracking account
No mandatory email, phone number or personal profile. Your wallet belongs to you.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Tariffia Wallet is non-custodial. Back up your recovery phrase and keep it offline. Never share it with anyone. Tariffia will never ask for your recovery phrase.

Swap and CrossPay are disabled in this release. They will return only when Tariffia's own backend and compliance requirements are ready.

Tariffia Wallet is open source. Review the code, report issues and follow development at https://github.com/komvalut/tariffia-wallet.

Take control. Keep your keys. Use Tariffia Wallet.
